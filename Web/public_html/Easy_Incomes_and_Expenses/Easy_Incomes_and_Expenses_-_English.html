Easy Incomes and Expenses will allow you to monitor your incomes and expenses easily. A smart and simple app.

A ledger for each year is available, so you can enter every operation you have made in your bank account. Your entries can be put into categories that will help you to understand where your money has been spent.

No additional features that are complicated or useless. A simple application that looks nice and elegant.
